The model

One model holds the whole villa.

Walls, slabs, glazing, railings and the pool are real objects with levels and sizes. Drawings, schedules and every image in this story come from the same model, so a change made once shows up everywhere.

Areas and levels

Every level, measured once.

Lift the floors apart and each level shows its rooms. Carpet areas for the brochure and the RERA statement are read from these rooms, so the sales sheet and the filing always agree.

Sun and shade

Know where the shade falls at four in the afternoon.

At Chennai's latitude the sun is high and hard, and the evening light decides whether a terrace gets used. Drag the hour and watch the pergola's shadow cross the deck.

Visualization

The blue-hour photograph, taken before there is a building.

Stills and short films come from the same model: the pool lit at dusk, the wall washers on, the terrace dressed. They are ready for the launch brochure and the hoardings while the site is still a plot.

On site, before construction

The family stands at the plot and watches their home rise.

The deck, the three floors, the boundary walls, the pool and the planting go up in sequence, on the plot and on the tablet in their hands. They see the height, the setbacks and the view from the gate before any work starts.

Interiors

Now walk through the glass, into the living hall.

Buyers spend their lives inside. Here the same specialist builds the hall layer by layer, in the order your site team will, so every decision is visible before it is built.

Columns, beams and slab come first.

The frame is modelled at its real sizes, so spans, beam depths and clear ceiling heights are known before anyone draws the interior.

Stage 3 of 7 · Services

Ducts and conduits, checked before plaster.

Air-conditioning ducts, light points, TV and floor sockets are modelled and checked against the beams. Clashes are fixed on screen, not by breaking finished walls.

How a week works

From your drawings to your client's phone.

  1. Brief

    You share the design and what the client needs to see this week.

  2. Model

    The specialist builds or updates the model in your standards.

  3. Visualize

    Images, films and VR scenes are produced from that model.

  4. Check

    ZERO.BIM reviews the work. You review it with your client.

  5. Share

    Links, walkthroughs and finish boards go out to buyers.
